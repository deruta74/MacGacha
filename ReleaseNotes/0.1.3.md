**Time left.** Downloads and full verification now show an estimate of the time left. Hover over the progress bar to see the downloaded size and speed.

**Long steps stay visible.** Steps without a percentage show how long they have been running, and MacGacha tells you when something is taking longer than usual.

**Choose a display.** In a game's settings you can pick the display it opens on. The main display is the default.

**Update notes.** This window now shows what changed in each version, right here instead of a web page.
