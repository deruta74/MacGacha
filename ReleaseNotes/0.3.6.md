# MacGacha 0.3.6

- **Play while another game downloads.** Press Play and MacGacha pauses the other game's download, then resumes it when you quit. Downloads you paused yourself stay paused. This also works when starting a game from the Dock or a shortcut.
- **Keep downloading during play.** Turn on **Download while playing** in Settings to keep another game's install, update or pre-download running. The speed limit during play starts at 10 MB/s and can be changed. A lower overall limit still applies. MacGacha also reduces how many files it downloads and assembles at once.
- **Clearer messages above Play.** Status hints now have a Liquid Glass background. Long launch errors wrap to fit the window and keep **Open Log** visible.
