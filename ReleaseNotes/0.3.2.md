# MacGacha 0.3.2

- **MacGacha Wine 11.22.** Our Wine moves to 11.22 for both DXMT and D3DMetal and arrives with automatic dependency updates. It includes the upstream certificate fix, so the separate crypt32 fix for Mainland China Genshin and ZZZ is no longer needed with it. Surround sound has a matching 11.22 module. Wine 11.20 stays available in Dependencies if you want to go back. Switching Wine starts a fresh prefix.
- **Download speed limit.** Settings now has a Download speed choice: Unlimited, or 5 to 100 MB/s. It applies to game installs, updates and pre-downloads, and a change takes effect right away, even during a download.
- **Notifications.** While MacGacha is in the background, it tells you when an install, update or repair finishes, fails or stops for disk space, and when a new game version or pre-download appears. Click a notification to open that game. A switch in Settings turns them off.
- **Storage.** A new Storage panel in the sidebar shows how much space each game, its Wine prefix and its pre-download take, plus Wine, logs and caches. "Can be freed" lists what MacGacha no longer needs, such as old runtime versions and prefixes of removed games, and removes each item only after you confirm.
- **Wish history export (opt-in).** Turn it on in Settings, open the wish history in the game once, then choose **Export Wish History…** in the game's menu. MacGacha saves your history as a UIGF 4.2 file that other wish trackers can import. It is off by default, and while it's off MacGacha doesn't read the game's history link at all.
- **Open Screenshots** in a game's menu opens the folder where the game saves its screenshots.
- **Reset Wine Prefix** in game settings recreates the game's Wine prefix on the next Play, which often helps when a game stops starting after a change. Game files and logins saved in the game folder are kept.
- **Play from the Dock.** Right-click MacGacha in the Dock to start an installed game.
- **Metal HUD options.** Choose what the HUD shows (Apple's standard set, frame rate only, a full set or your own selection), where it sits on screen and how large it is, separately for each game.
- **A tidier launcher.** The game's version and your play time sit in two small capsules above Play, the line above them only appears when there's something to say, the video switch only shows on animated backgrounds, and an available pre-download is a round icon beside Play.
