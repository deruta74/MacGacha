- Fix Genshin Impact and Zenless Zone Zero startup failures by applying a Windows 11 Wine profile before launch.
- Automatically update existing and new prefixes for both Global and Mainland China editions, independently of the Compatibility patches setting. Star Rail keeps its existing profile.

Verified in Global Genshin 7.2 with DXMT and D3DMetal, and Global ZZZ 3.3 with D3DMetal (DirectX 12 and surround sound enabled). Mainland China gameplay has not yet been verified.
