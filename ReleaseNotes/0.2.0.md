**14 languages.** MacGacha now speaks English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese. It follows your Mac by default, and you can pick a language in Settings. Game art and news follow it too.

**Backgrounds like HoYoPlay.** The launcher shows each game's backgrounds, videos included, in HoYoPlay's order, with Version Highlights and the latest events. Every time you open MacGacha, the next background appears. Point at the dots to switch.

**Genshin Impact frame-rate unlock.** An opt-in switch in Genshin's settings raises the frame limit to 120 FPS or a value you choose. It's off by default, and the first time you turn it on MacGacha explains the account risk. Thanks to genshin-fps-unlock for the method.

**Star Rail at 120 FPS.** A switch in Star Rail's settings unlocks 120 FPS. The frame limit also offers 90.

**Surround sound.** Genshin Impact and Zenless Zone Zero can play in surround, with Spatial Audio on AirPods and other supported headphones. Turn it on in the game's settings.

**Game Mode.** macOS turns on Game Mode for the games themselves, not for the launcher. Each game shows up with its own name and icon. You can turn this off in Settings.

**Check for game updates.** A game's menu can now check for a new version right away.

**Smoother video.** Background videos play with the Mac's own decoder and use less memory.
