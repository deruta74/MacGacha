**Smaller game updates.** Updates now download HoYoverse's official patches when a game offers them, just like HoYoPlay. A Genshin Impact update can be about a third of its previous size. Games without patches update as before.

**Pre-download.** When the next version opens for pre-download, a button above Play shows its size. The files download in the background while your installed version stays playable, and the update installs them when the new version is released. You can pause, resume or delete the pre-download at any time. Pressing Play pauses it.
