**Dependencies.** The Runtimes panel is now called Dependencies and has a new design. Components are grouped into Runtimes, Compatibility and Extras, and each one has its own card that explains what it does, shows whether it's installed and offers a single button to set it up or update it.

**Easier rollback.** When a component has more than one version, they're listed on its card with a check mark on the one in use. Switch to another installed version with Use, or pin and remove versions from the … menu.
